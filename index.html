Two different pipelines: -LDAPFilter and Search-LDAP evaluate LDAP expressions in the simulated directory; the PowerShell | operator passes JavaScript objects between cmdlets. Neither opens a real LDAP connection.

Simulated changes: Set-ADUser, Enable-ADAccount, Disable-ADAccount and group membership commands modify in-memory data only. Click Reset sandbox to restore examples.

Limits: This is a deliberately incomplete PowerShell and LDAP implementation. No real AD server, no authentication, AD replication, ACLs, LDAP bind, network, or OS execution. Do not use it to validate production scripts.
